## 2. Engineering and Consulting Services

Tayoca publicly groups its work into three practices:

### Cloud Economics and FinOps
- Review cloud spending, workloads and cost drivers.
- Identify opportunities to reduce waste without undermining reliability.
- Produce a prioritized optimization backlog, estimated impact, assumptions, owners and a validation method.
- The public website lists a cloud-cost engagement at **$5,000 flat fee**; implementation may be separately scoped.

### Platform Reliability and DevSecOps
- Support platform architecture, delivery controls, security practices, reliability and recovery.
- Emphasize acceptance criteria, validation, runbooks, ownership and handover.
- The public website lists a platform engagement starting at **$8,000** on a fixed-scope basis.

### AI and Workflow Automation
- Identify repetitive processes and design bounded automation pilots.
- Cover integration design, observable failure handling, acceptance tests and human escalation.
- The public website lists automation pilots **from $5,000**.

**Pricing note:** These are publicly displayed website figures, not quotes or guarantees. Final scope and commitments require an agreed engagement.

**Source:** https://tayoca.com/services.html

## 3. Public Delivery Process

Tayoca describes a four-stage engagement model:

1. **Understand:** Clarify the current problem, operating decision and available evidence.
2. **Bound:** Agree inputs, exclusions, acceptance criteria and the smallest useful intervention.
3. **Change:** Implement the agreed solution with appropriate controls, observability and handoff.
4. **Verify:** Compare results against the baseline and document improvements and remaining limitations.

Additional publicly stated practices:
- Distinguish facts, assumptions and modeled outcomes.
- Treat recovery plans and runbooks as delivery requirements.
- Apply human approval to sensitive decisions and material publication.
- Document ownership and change control rather than allowing scope to expand implicitly.

**Sources:** https://tayoca.com/services.html ; https://tayoca.com/about.html
